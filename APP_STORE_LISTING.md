# App Store listing — Clicker – Remote for Roku

Text to paste into App Store Connect. Each block is one field; the heading
gives Apple's character limit. Written 2026-11-17 for the weekly + lifetime
model (10 free key presses, weekly plan with a 3-day free trial, lifetime
unlock). If any of those numbers change in the app, change them here too.

Rules this text follows, so edits keep to them:

- Never "one price", "no subscription", or "free" as a description of the app.
- No dollar amounts. Prices differ by country and App Store shows them itself.
- No Roku logos. "Roku" appears only to say what the app works with.
- The Terms of Use link stays in every description (required for subscriptions).

## App Review notes

Paste into App Review Information → Notes. Replace the video link first.

```text
Clicker is a remote control for Roku streaming players and Roku TVs. It sends commands over the local network using Roku's External Control Protocol (HTTP on port 8060). It needs a Roku device on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPhone, so the remote itself cannot be used without one. A demo video of the app controlling a real TV is here: [UNLISTED VIDEO LINK]

SIGN-IN
None. The app has no accounts.

REACHING THE PURCHASE SCREEN WITHOUT A ROKU
1. Launch the app and finish the short introduction.
2. On the device list, tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.
3. Tap "See Plans".

HOW PURCHASES WORK
The app is free to download. The first 10 key presses sent to a Roku are free. After that the app shows two plans, and either one unlocks the whole app:
- Clicker Weekly (com.catchergriffin.clicker.weekly): auto-renewable subscription, 1 week, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.
- Clicker Lifetime (com.catchergriffin.clicker.unlock): non-consumable, one payment.
Restore Purchases is on the plans screen and in Settings. Manage Subscription is in Settings once a subscription exists. The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are on the plans screen and in the App Store description.

LOCAL NETWORK PERMISSION
iOS asks for Local Network access the first time the app looks for a Roku. The app cannot find or control a device without it. Nothing is sent outside the local network.

NAME AND TRADEMARKS
"for Roku" in the name describes compatibility only. The app uses no Roku logos or artwork, and it states in Settings and in Help that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roku, Inc. Channel icons in the channel list are loaded at runtime from the user's own Roku device and are not bundled with the app.

DATA
The app collects no data. It has no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.
```
