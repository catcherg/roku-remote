Questions, answered

Does it work with all Rokus?

Clicker works with any Roku device on your Wi-Fi network — streaming sticks, boxes, and Roku TVs. Volume and power controls appear when your device is a Roku TV.

We invite testers from the waitlist in small batches to keep the experience premium and the feedback loop tight. Reserve a spot, and you'll get an email when it's your turn.

Is it really one-time?

Really. After a 7-day free trial you pay once, and every future update is included. Remote apps that charge weekly are the problem Clicker exists to fix.
