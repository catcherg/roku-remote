Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

The Clicker app collects nothing. This website stores your email only so we can manage the waitlist and beta. We run no analytics, set no tracking cookies, and never sell data.

The Clicker app

The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It talks only to your Roku device, directly over your local Wi-Fi network. No data about you, your device, or what you watch ever leaves your network or reaches our servers — we don't have servers the app could talk to.

This website

If you create an account on getclicker.me, we store:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password
  • An optional display name, if you provide one
  • Your waitlist position and beta invite status

This data is used solely to communicate with you about the waitlist, the beta, and the app's launch. It is stored with Supabase, our authentication and database provider, and protected by row-level security so only you can access your own record.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts of any kind
  • No advertising, no ad networks
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone
  • No cookies beyond the single session cookie that keeps you logged in

Deleting your data

Email us and we will delete your account and all associated data. Self-serve account deletion is coming to your account page soon.
